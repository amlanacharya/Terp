## FAQ

### General

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. Your data is stored locally on your machine. We have no access to it. Regular backups ensure you never lose data.

**Q: Do I need internet?**
A: No. TravelERP Lite works completely offline. Internet is only needed for updates and license activation.

**Q: Can I sync across multiple computers?**
A: Yes. Use Network Mode to share the database across multiple computers on your local network.

**Q: What happens after my subscription ends?**
A: The app switches to read-only mode. You can view and export data, but not create new records. Renew your subscription to continue.

### Technical

**Q: Will it slow down my computer?**
A: No. TravelERP Lite is optimized for performance and uses minimal resources (<500MB RAM).

**Q: Can I switch from another system?**
A: Yes. Import from Excel, CSV, or most other systems. We also offer paid migration assistance.

**Q: What if I change my computer?**
A: Install TravelERP Lite on the new computer and reactivate with your license key. Restore from backup to get your data.

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge?**
A: No. The first-run wizard guides you through setup. No technical skills required.

### Licensing

**Q: Is it really one-time purchase?**
A: Yes. Buy a subscription (monthly/quarterly/annual) and use until it expires. No recurring payments unless you choose to renew.

**Q: Can I transfer my license?**
A: Licenses are tied to hardware. Changes may require reactivation. Contact support for assistance.

**Q: Do I get updates?**
A: Yes. All updates within your subscription period are free. Major version updates may require renewal.
